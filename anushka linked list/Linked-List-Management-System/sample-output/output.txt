LINKED LIST MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Example test sequence:
1. Choose Singly Linked List.
2. Choose Insert at End and enter 40.
3. Choose Insert at End and enter 10.
4. Choose Insert at End and enter 30.
5. Choose Insert at End and enter 20.
6. Choose Display.

Output:
40 -> 10 -> 30 -> 20 -> NULL

Choose Sort:
List sorted in ascending order.
10 -> 20 -> 30 -> 40 -> NULL

Choose Reverse:
List reversed.
40 -> 30 -> 20 -> 10 -> NULL

For a Doubly Linked List containing 10, 20, 30, 40:
Forward:  NULL <-> 10 <-> 20 <-> 30 <-> 40 <-> NULL
Backward: NULL <-> 40 <-> 30 <-> 20 <-> 10 <-> NULL
